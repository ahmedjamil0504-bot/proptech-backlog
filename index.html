Weekly Priority List

Rank the team's most important initiatives and activities for each week.

Enter a rank number when adding an item or editing a row. Rank #1 is the highest priority. Drag rows to reorder. Use ↑ / ↓ for keyboard-friendly adjustments. Ranking is independent of the normal backlog priority field.

Activities by status

Drag cards within or between columns to set their position and status. Ranking here is independent of Weekly Priorities.
